About TidyToolbox

Small tools, built one at a time.

TidyToolbox is a small collection of free web tools, each built to do exactly one job well: turning photos into a PDF, converting an image from one format to another, generating a QR code, putting together a resume. No accounts, no dashboards, no features you didn't ask for.

The idea came from a simple annoyance: needing to do something small and specific online, like converting a photo to a PDF, and landing on a page buried in pop ups, forced sign ups, or a dozen irrelevant "features." TidyToolbox is an attempt to do the opposite: open the tool, use it, get your file, done.

How it's funded

These tools are free to use and supported by ads on the page. That's what keeps them free and keeps them online: there's no premium tier hiding better functionality behind a paywall.

What's next

More tools get added as they're built. If there's a small, specific tool you find yourself needing over and over, that's usually exactly the kind of thing that ends up here.
