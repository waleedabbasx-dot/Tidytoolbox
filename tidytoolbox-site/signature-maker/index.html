A free online signature maker for a transparent PNG

Uploading a photo removes the background around your ink so what's left is a clean, transparent PNG you can drop onto documents, contracts or emails without a white box around it. Drawing mode lets you sign directly with a trackpad, mouse or touchscreen, exporting straight to a transparent PNG with no photo needed at all.

Everything runs locally in your browser. Nothing you upload or draw is sent to a server, and there's no sign up needed to use it.

A signature with a transparent background sits cleanly on top of any document — no white box covering the text or the signature line underneath. That's what makes the PNG this tool produces drop straight into:

  • Word and Google Docs letters and contracts
  • PDF forms, agreements, and invoices via any PDF editor
  • Email signatures and scanned documents that need signing

Two ways to make your digital signature

Draw it: sign in the box with your mouse, trackpad, or a finger on a touchscreen — signing on a phone often feels the most natural. Or upload it: sign on paper the way you always do, photograph it, and the tool removes the paper background to leave just the ink as a transparent PNG. The upload route keeps your real, legally-familiar signature rather than a mouse-drawn approximation.

Because everything runs in your browser, your signature — sensitive data by any definition — is never uploaded, stored, or seen by a server.

Is this signature maker free?

Yes. Creating and downloading a signature PNG here is completely free, with no account and no limit on how many you make.

Is this a true vector image?

No. Full photo to vector tracing needs a much heavier process and isn't reliable for handwriting. What this tool does is remove the background and export a crisp transparent PNG, which is what almost every signature tool actually delivers.

Will the signature have a white box around it?

No. The output is a PNG with a transparent background, so it drops cleanly onto any document or colored background.

Can I sign with just a mouse if I don't have a trackpad?

Yes. The draw mode works the same way with a mouse, trackpad or touchscreen.

How do I make my signature background transparent?

Upload a photo of your signature and the tool strips out the paper background automatically, leaving only the ink strokes as a transparent PNG. If you draw your signature instead, the background is transparent from the start.

Can I use this signature in Word or Google Docs?

Yes. Insert the downloaded PNG as an image and place it on the signature line. Because the background is transparent, it sits over the line and surrounding text without a white box.

Are drawn or uploaded electronic signatures valid?

In many countries an electronic signature image is accepted for everyday agreements, but the rules vary by country and document type, and some documents require more formal e-signing or a wet ink signature. If a document is important, check what your situation requires.
