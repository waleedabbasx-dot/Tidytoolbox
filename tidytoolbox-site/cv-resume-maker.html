A free resume builder with 10 templates, with or without a photo

Pick from 10 generic designs, six plain layouts and four that include a photo, then fill in your details section by section: personal info, a short summary, work experience, education, and skills. The preview updates as you type, so what you see is close to what ends up in the downloaded PDF. When it looks right, hit "Download PDF" to get a clean file ready to send or upload to a job application.

Everything runs in your browser. Your details and photo are kept only in this browser's local storage so your draft survives a refresh, and nothing is ever sent to a server.

Most online resume builders let you do all the work, then hide the download behind a subscription. This one doesn't: the PDF download is free, every template is free, and there is no account, trial, or credit card step between you and your finished resume.

  • 10 templates covering both styles: photo CV layouts common in Europe, the Middle East, and Asia, and no-photo resume layouts preferred in the US, UK, and Canada
  • Live preview — the document rebuilds as you type, so what you see is exactly the PDF you download
  • Simple, text-focused layouts without heavy graphics or tables — the kind of clean structure that automated resume scanners handle best
  • Private by design — your name, work history, and contact details stay in your browser and are never uploaded or stored on a server

Make a professional CV in a few minutes

Fill in the form section by section — personal details, work experience, education, skills — pick the template that fits the job you're applying for, and export. Because nothing is locked, you can come back any time, tweak the content, switch templates, and download a fresh PDF for every application.

Is this resume builder free?

Yes. Building and downloading your resume here is completely free, with no account and no watermark on the PDF.

Do I have to use a photo?

No. Six of the ten designs are plain, text only layouts. A photo is only requested if you pick one of the four designs built around one.

Is my information or photo stored anywhere?

Only in your own browser's local storage, so your draft is still there if you close the tab and come back. Nothing is uploaded or sent anywhere.

Can I add more than one job or school?

Yes. Use "Add experience" or "Add education" to add as many entries as you need, and remove any entry you don't want with the small remove link on that entry.

Is this resume builder really free to download?

Yes. Unlike many resume sites, the PDF download is not behind a paywall. You build the resume, you download it, and no account or payment is ever requested.

Should my CV include a photo?

It depends on where you're applying. Photo CVs are common in much of Europe, the Middle East, and Asia, while employers in the US, UK, and Canada generally expect no photo. This builder includes templates for both, so pick whichever matches the market.

Is my personal information stored anywhere?

No. Everything you type stays in your browser on your own device. Nothing is uploaded, so your work history and contact details are never sitting on someone else's server.
