What this policy covers

This policy explains what information TidyToolbox collects when you use our tools (Photo → PDF, Image Format Converter, QR Code Generator, CV/Resume Maker) and this website generally.

Your files stay on your device

The tools on this site process your photos, images, and documents directly in your browser. Files you upload to a tool are not sent to our servers and are not stored by us: they exist only in your browser's memory while you're using the tool, and are gone once you close or refresh the page.

Cookies and advertising

This site shows ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites, and to measure ad performance.

  • You can learn more about how Google uses data from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • If you're in a region covered by GDPR or similar regulations, you may be shown a consent option before ads are personalized.

Analytics

We may use analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) to understand overall traffic and usage patterns, for example, which tools are used most, and general visitor counts. This data is aggregated and is not used to identify you personally.

Information we don't collect

We don't require accounts, passwords, or payment information to use any tool on this site. We don't ask for your name, email, or contact details unless you choose to email us directly.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time as tools are added or requirements change. The "last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
