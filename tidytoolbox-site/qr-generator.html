How this free QR code generator works

Type or paste whatever you want to encode: a website link, a short message, contact details, or Wi Fi info, into the box above. As you type, a QR code is generated instantly right in your browser. Pick a size, then download it as a PNG to use however you need, whether that's printing it, adding it to a flyer or business card, or sharing it digitally.

Everything happens locally on your device. Nothing you type is uploaded to a server, so it works just as well for a private note as it does for a public link.

The codes this tool makes are static QR codes: your link or text is encoded directly into the pattern itself. That means they never expire, there are no scan limits, and they don't depend on this site or any account staying alive — a code you print on a poster today will still scan in ten years. Many "free" QR code generators quietly create dynamic codes tied to a paid account that stop working after a trial; nothing like that happens here.

Typical uses include QR codes for a website link, a menu, a Google review page, a portfolio, an event RSVP, a Wi-Fi info card, product packaging, business cards, and flyers. Anything you can express as a URL or plain text can become a code.

Make QR codes in bulk from a spreadsheet

The batch mode turns this into a bulk QR code generator: export your spreadsheet as a CSV, upload it, and get a whole set of codes at once — one per row. It's built for the situations where making codes one by one is painful: name badges and check-in links for an event, table cards for a restaurant, asset tags for inventory, or per-product links for packaging.

Will this QR code expire?

No. The QR code encodes your text directly, so it works for as long as the underlying content (like a link) stays valid. There's no account or subscription involved.

What can I put into a QR code?

Anything text based: a URL, plain text, an email address, a phone number, or Wi Fi credentials in the standard format. Most phone cameras can scan the result without an extra app.

Is this QR code generator free?

Yes. Generating and downloading QR codes here is completely free, with no account and no limit on how many you create.

Is there a limit to how much text I can use?

QR codes can hold a few thousand characters, but shorter text produces a simpler, easier to scan code. If your text is too long, you'll see an error and can shorten it.

Can I generate many QR codes at once?

Yes. Switch to "Batch from CSV," upload a spreadsheet with one row per code, and download every QR code as a single ZIP file. Handy for event tickets, table numbers, or tagging inventory in bulk.

Do QR codes made here expire?

No. These are static QR codes, so the data is stored in the pattern itself. They keep working forever, with unlimited scans, and don't depend on any account or subscription.

Can I use these QR codes commercially?

Yes. Codes you generate are yours to use anywhere, including business cards, packaging, menus, posters, and marketing material, at no cost.

How do I generate many QR codes at once?

Use the CSV batch mode: put one link or text entry per row in a spreadsheet, save it as a CSV file, and upload it. The tool generates a QR code for every row so you can download the whole set in one go.
